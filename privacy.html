2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you:

  • Submit a contact or lead form (name, business name, phone, email)

  • Interact with QR codes, NFC-enabled profiles, or contact-sharing flows

We also collect limited technical data automatically, including IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referral source, to improve our services and understand how visitors use our platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry or booking request
  • To send you SMS messages and emails you have consented to receive

4. SMS Communications

If you opt in to SMS, we will send you messages related to your account, bookings, service updates, and platform offers. Message frequency varies. Standard message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance. See our SMS Terms for full details.

We do not sell or share your phone number or SMS consent status with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers — such as Twilio (SMS delivery), Brevo (email delivery), Supabase (database), and Vercel (hosting) — who process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements
  • Business partners — only with your consent, such as the specific business whose page you interacted with

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. Lead and contact data is retained for up to 3 years. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us at info@cybercheckinc.com.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@cybercheckinc.com.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use standard analytics tools to track page visits and interactions. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.
