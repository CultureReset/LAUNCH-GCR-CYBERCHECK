Program Description

CyberCheck LLC operates Gulf Coast Radar and sends SMS messages to users who opt in through website forms, booking requests, QR code scans, or NFC-enabled contact sharing. Messages include booking confirmations, reminders, lead follow-ups, contact-sharing notifications, review requests, account notifications, and occasional promotional updates related to Gulf Coast Radar business services and local offers.

How to Opt In

You opt in to receive SMS messages by submitting a form on gulfcoastalhub.com or any CyberCheck-powered page and checking the required SMS consent checkbox before submission. You may also opt in through booking forms, contact-sharing forms, QR code flows, and NFC-enabled profile pages where consent language is displayed before submission.

By opting in, you confirm that you are the account holder or authorized user of the phone number provided.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your activity and account status. You may receive messages related to:

  • Form submission confirmations and follow-ups
  • Booking confirmations and reminders
  • Account and service notifications
  • Platform updates and promotional offers
  • Review requests after a completed booking or service

Message and Data Rates

Msg & data rates may apply. Standard carrier rates for SMS messages will apply depending on your mobile plan. CyberCheck LLC does not charge for SMS messages directly.

How to Opt Out

You can cancel SMS messages at any time. Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message. You will not receive further SMS messages unless you opt in again.
